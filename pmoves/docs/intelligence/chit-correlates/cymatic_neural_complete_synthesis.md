#### 1. Acoustic Neural Networks: Identifying Design Principles and Exploring Architectures

- **Authors**: (not extracted from search)

CHIT claims cymatic patterns emerge from standard NN computations; this paper implements NN computations using physical wave media.

## Semantic Trap Analysis

Four systematic semantic traps explain why even reasonable query formulations fail to surface relevant work:

| Trap | Query Term | Captured By | Why It Fails |
|------|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| 1 | `modal analysis transformer` | Electrical power transformers | "Transformer" in vibration analysis = power grid device, not AI architecture |
| 2 | `eigenmode patterns deep learning` | Optical fiber mode decomposition | "Eigenmode" in photonics = light propagation modes, overwhelms cross-domain signal |
| 3 | `wave physics neural network` | PINNs (Physics-Informed NNs) | "Wave physics" implies NN solves wave equations, not that wave patterns emerge from NN |
| 4 | `multi-modal transformer` | Medical imaging modalities | "Modal" = CT/MRI/X-ray, not vibration modes |

These traps mean the bridging vocabulary simply does not exist in any established literature. The communities that use these terms have no overlap with the community that studies NN activation geometry.

## Why the White Space Persists: Four Disconnected Communities

The absence of work at this intersection is explained by four communities operating in incompatible linguistic frames:

1. **Physics-domain ML researchers** (PINNs, wave equation solvers): Use NNs to STUDY waves. Waves are the subject, not the metaphor for NN internals. No incentive to look for wave patterns in activations.

2. **ML activation researchers** (representational geometry, mechanistic interpretability): Use linear algebra, information geometry, and topological data analysis frames. Lack wave physics vocabulary entirely. Work in Euclidean/Riemannian manifolds without invoking resonance, modes, or interference.

3. **Cymatics enthusiasts/practitioners**: Use AI as a tool FOR cymatics (pattern generation, parameter optimization). Cymatics is the subject; AI is the instrument. Never reverse the lens.

4. **Philosophy/bloggers**: Use wave metaphors for AI interaction patterns but explicitly disclaim technical physics interpretation. The metaphor is consciously non-literal.

No community has incentive to bridge these frames. The vocabulary for describing "cymatic patterns in NN activations" literally does not exist in any published work.
