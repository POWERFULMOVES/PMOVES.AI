### Video: "Fable 5 + Karpathy's LLM Wiki is Basically Cheating"
**Channel:** Nate Herk | AI Automation (846K subscribers)
**Duration:** 14:35

**Published:** July 4, 2026

#### Video Chapter Breakdown:

| Timestamp | Chapter | Content Summary |
|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| 0:00 | The LLM Wiki Demo | Demo of Fable 5 ingesting and querying structured knowledge from an LLM Wiki |
| 1:13 | What Fable Does With the Data | How Fable processes wiki data into a queryable knowledge graph |

| 7:59 | Flat vs Structured Wikis | Tradeoffs between flat markdown dumps and structured hierarchical wikis |
| 9:49 | Ingesting Two Sources | Multi-source knowledge ingestion — combining different information streams |
| 12:38 | Why It Works: Routing | **The routing layer** — how queries are directed to the right knowledge subgraph |

#### Key Technical Concepts from Video:

1. **LLM Wiki Architecture:** A structured knowledge base built in markdown/Obsidian that serves as persistent memory for AI agents. Inspired by Andrej Karpathy's public wiki approach.

2. **Fable 5 as Knowledge Router:** Fable doesn't just store knowledge — it routes queries to the appropriate wiki sections, enabling context-aware retrieval. This is essentially a **knowledge mesh** with intelligent routing.

3. **Multi-Wiki AI OS Pattern:** The video describes building an "AI OS" composed of multiple specialized wikis (business, technical, personal) that work together. This is directly analogous to PMOVES's multi-agent architecture where different agents hold different domain expertise.

4. **Structured vs Flat Knowledge:** The emphasis on structured wikis over flat dumps mirrors PMOVES's MOF architecture — porous, interconnected knowledge structures with defined pathways.

5. **The Routing Problem:** At 12:38, the video explains "Why It Works: Routing" — this is the critical insight. The intelligence isn't just in the knowledge storage but in the **routing mechanism** that determines which knowledge to activate for which query. This maps directly to PMOVES's agent orchestration layer.

#### Why This Video Matters for DARKXSIDE's Persona:

- **Validates the PMOVES architectural direction:** Multi-wiki AI OS with intelligent routing is exactly what PMOVES is building
- **Shows practical implementation details:** Obsidian + Fable provides a concrete reference for how knowledge structuring works
- **Demonstrates the "cheating" effect:** Structured knowledge makes AI systems dramatically more capable — just as MOF architecture would
- **Connects Karpathy → Fable → PMOVES:** Shows the lineage of ideas from thought leader (Karpathy) to tool (Fable) to platform (PMOVES)
