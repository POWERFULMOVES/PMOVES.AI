Fordham Hill

Shareholder Briefing · Prepared 2026-10-10 · pmoves.ai LIVE measured, not modeled

The pitch

Fordham Hill is a live-piloted, machine-verifiable model of a modernized housing cooperative: residents pool their internet through measured community infrastructure instead of retail ISP contracts, costs split transparently, every contribution credited on a tamper-evident public ledger — and governance that stays one-resident-one-vote no matter what anyone contributes. The infrastructure already exists and works. As of this week, the public proof site is pmoves.ai.

The governance firewall

The question everyone asks: “can a big contributor buy the vote?” No — by construction. Contribution tokens never translate into vote weight. Settlement ≠ voting power. One-unit-one-vote is supreme, written into the bylaws scaffold (Clause L), not promised. Every write is signed (CHIT) and publicly auditable.

Objection handling

“Is this crypto?” — No sale, no speculation. Attribution units stay internal; settlement is USD-stable and counsel-gated.

“Who controls it?” — The co-op. Agent-operated infrastructure, but every write is signed and auditable; governance weight can't be bought.

“Cost to residents?” — Fleet is $30/mo total today. The pilot measures the real split before dues change.

“What if it fails?” — Overlay, not replacement. Fail closed = today's internet, unchanged.
