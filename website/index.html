How a request moves through PMOVES.

Four steps. Every one of them is a design choice, not a policy.

  1. 01

    Arrive over mesh, not through Big Telco.

    Requests reach the node over OpenMANET Wi-Fi HaLow (902–928 MHz, 1–3 km range, megabit-class bandwidth) or a Tailscale/Starlink uplink. If the ISP goes dark, the block keeps talking.

  2. 02

    Retrieve context with Hi-RAG v2.

    Hybrid retrieval: Qdrant vectors + Neo4j graph + Meilisearch full-text, reranked by a cross-encoder. Your encrypted-at-rest corpus stays on your node — no round trip to somebody else's cloud.

  3. 03

    Spawn a sterile microVM — compute, respond.

    A fresh Firecracker microVM boots in 150 ms, runs the LLM call, and hands back the response. The agent orchestrator (Agent Zero + ClawZ) coordinates multi-step tasks over NATS — nothing sticks between jobs.

  4. 04

    Destroy the VM. Log audit metadata. That's it.

    The microVM is torn down. Only signed audit metadata is kept (who asked, when, for how long, with what policy — never the content). Zero-retention by architecture.
