### A2: Specific Mechanisms (What Is Actually Happening Physically/Mathematically)

**Mechanism 1: Cosmological Invariant Set Postulate**
The universe does not evolve TOWARD a fractal attractor (as in standard chaos theory where arbitrary initial conditions are attracted). Instead, the universe has ALWAYS been evolving ON this fractal geometry and always will be. The set is called "invariant" because if a state is on it at time T, it was on it at all past times and will be on it at all future times. The key distinction from an attractor: states that are NOT on the set are not "outside and being attracted" — they are inconsistent with the laws of physics entirely. This reframes physics from an initial-value-problem paradigm (specify initial conditions, evolve forward) to a geometric paradigm (the geometry of the invariant set IS the physics). [08:41-15:48]

**Mechanism 2: Counterfactual Definiteness Violation via Invariant Set Constraint**
Bell's theorem implicitly assumes "measurement independence": you can hold hidden variables fixed and freely vary measurement settings, and all resulting states are physically consistent. Palmer shows this is false for invariant set models. If you actually perform measurement A (position), the state lies on the invariant set. The counterfactual state "what would I have measured had I done B (momentum) instead" may lie OFF the invariant set — making it not merely unknown but physically impossible. This is not conspiracy (no signaling between particles); it is a geometric constraint. The "millionth digit" example: changing that digit in a pseudo-random number generator setting takes the entire world state off the invariant set — "all the galaxies would vanish in a puff of metaphysical smoke" [28:32-28:45]. [15:03-24:06]

**Mechanism 3: P-Adic Distance Function for Fractal Robustness**
Real numbers use the Euclidean metric (fingers approaching = distance decreasing). P-adic numbers use a fundamentally different metric. On a fractal invariant set described by p-adic numbers: two points ON the set can have small p-adic distance, but a point in the GAP between them has VERY LARGE p-adic distance to points on the set. This makes the invariant set scheme "robust" from the p-adic perspective — you can't make tiny perturbations that nudged you slightly off the set, because p-adically, "slightly off" is actually VERY FAR away. This resolves the fine-tuning objection ("you're not even allowing me to change the millionth digit"). [30:01-31:30]

**Mechanism 4: Rational vs. Irrational Hilbert Space Discretization**
An alternative formulation of the same idea: if you discretize Hilbert space using rational numbers (digits 0 to P-1), then when you try to define the quantum states of entangled particles under counterfactual measurement settings, the required Hilbert state vectors have strictly IRRATIONAL amplitudes or phases. These irrational states are forbidden in the discretized space — they correspond to states off the invariant set. This captures the same counterfactual indefiniteness but framed in the language practicing quantum theorists already use. Palmer believes this "rational quantum mechanics" is equivalent to invariant set theory, though technical details remain to be fully worked out (analogous to Feynman-Schwinger needing Dyson to prove equivalence). [46:04-54:04]

**Mechanism 5: Hilbert Space Continuity as Artifact of Misidentified Fundamentality**
In quantum mechanics, Hilbert spaces must be continuous (infinite-dimensional over complex numbers) because vector addition must be closed: adding two quantum states must yield another quantum state. Discretizing breaks this closure. BUT this continuity is only required if Hilbert states are FUNDAMENTAL. If there is a deeper deterministic layer (the invariant set), then Hilbert states are merely statistical representations of uncertainty — and the continuity requirement becomes a property of the representation, not of reality. At the deeper deterministic level, p-adic numbers provide closure under addition and multiplication on the fractal. [46:04-51:18]

**Mechanism 6: Barry's Billiard Ball — Computational Irreducibility and Holism**
