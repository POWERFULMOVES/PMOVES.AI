### A2: Specific Mechanisms (What Is Actually Happening Physically/Biologically)

**Mechanism 1: Gap Junction Electrical Synapses as Tissue-Level Communication Bus**
All cells in the body possess ion channels that establish voltage gradients across their membranes. Most cells are connected to neighbors via gap junctions — protein channels that form direct electrical synapses between adjacent cells, allowing ions and small signaling molecules to pass without synaptic vesicle release. This creates a continuous electrical network across tissue, analogous to a bus topology. The spatial pattern of resting membrane potentials across this network encodes anatomical target states. This architecture predates neurons — it exists from the time of bacterial biofilms. [23:06-25:03]

**Mechanism 2: Voltage Prepattern Preceding Gene Expression (Electric Face)**
In the frog embryo, BEFORE any gene expression that regionalizes the ectoderm into facial structures, the spatial pattern of resting membrane potentials already encodes where the right eye, left eye, and mouth will form. The voltage pattern is upstream of gene expression — it DETERMINES which genes turn on in which locations, not the reverse. Changing the voltage pattern changes the anatomical outcome even though the genome is untouched. This is demonstrated by injecting mRNA encoding specific ion channels (e.g., potassium channels) to alter local membrane potential, which induces ectopic eye formation. [26:28-27:16, 30:12-30:52]

**Mechanism 3: Cell-to-Cell Voltage Contagion via Physical Contact**
Individual cells in vitro maintain stable voltage states (shown as colors via voltage-sensitive dyes). When a cell at one voltage state is physically touched by a cell at a different voltage state, the contacted cell's voltage immediately changes to match the touching cell. This is not chemical signaling — it is direct electrical coupling through gap junctions that open upon contact. The cell then joins the new collective and begins participating in whatever morphogenetic program the group is executing. This is the physical mechanism by which a few "teacher" cells recruit unmodified "student" cells into a collective construction project. [29:24-29:52]

**Mechanism 4: Large-Scale Error Detection Without Local Damage (Tail-to-Limb Grafting)**
When a tail is grafted to the flank of an amphibian, the tail cells at the tip are locally undamaged — there is no injury, no wound healing signal. Yet over time these tail cells remodel into fingers. The mechanism: a body-plan-level error detection system recognizes that "having a tail HERE is not what is supposed to be happening." This large-scale error signal propagates down to the molecular level, instructing cells to change their behavior to conform to the global anatomical plan. Individual tail cells do not "know" what a finger is — they respond to a body-plan-level error signal that doesn't exist at their scale. [16:57-18:09]

**Mechanism 5: Cancer Suppression as Electrical Veto Negotiation**
