## RISK ANALYSIS & MITIGATION

### Revenue Risks

**Risk 1: UN pilot doesn't convert (Year 2)**

- **Probability:** Low (formal mandate exists; no alternatives)  
- **Impact:** High ($750K Year 2 revenue miss)  
- **Mitigation:** Diversify Year 2 revenue with 3-4 disaster/medical contracts ($300K-500K total)

**Risk 2: Community adoption slower than projected**

- **Probability:** Medium (community engagement is hard)  
- **Impact:** Medium (Year 3-4 revenue reduced by $500K-1M)  
- **Mitigation:** Over-recruit Year 1 pilot (75 families vs. 50 target); prove model early

**Risk 3: Grant funding delays (Year 1-2)**

- **Probability:** Medium (grant timelines unpredictable)  
- **Impact:** High (cash flow crunch if >6 month delay)  
- **Mitigation:** Apply to 5-7 funders simultaneously; accept first $500K secured; maintain $100K personal reserve

### Cost Risks

**Risk 4: Infrastructure costs higher than projected**

- **Probability:** Medium (cloud pricing volatile)  
- **Impact:** Low-Medium (+$50K-100K/year)  
- **Mitigation:** Negotiate AWS/GCP volume discounts; use Hostinger VPS where possible; pass node hardware costs to partners

**Risk 5: Personnel costs escalate (talent market competitive)**

- **Probability:** Medium (AI engineers in demand)  
- **Impact:** Medium (+$100K-200K/year by Year 3)  
- **Mitigation:** Use token grants ($CAT) as part of comp; prioritize culture/mission fit; remote-first reduces salary benchmarks

### Operational Risks

**Risk 6: Security breach / data leak (catastrophic for zero-retention claim)**

- **Probability:** Low (architecture designed for this)  
- **Impact:** Catastrophic (destroys credibility, UN contracts at risk)  
- **Mitigation:** $50K/year R&D budget for continuous audits; third-party penetration testing quarterly; bug bounty program

**Risk 7: DAO governance deadlock (community can't agree on decisions)**

- **Probability:** Low-Medium (two-house model reduces risk)  
- **Impact:** Medium (delays strategic decisions)  
- **Mitigation:** Constitutional bylaws with tie-breaker mechanisms; founder retains strategic veto in Year 1-2; transition to full community by Year 3
