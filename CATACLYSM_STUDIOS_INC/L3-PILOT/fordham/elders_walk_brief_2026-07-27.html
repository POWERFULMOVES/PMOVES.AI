Fordham Hill Community Pilot — Walk Brief

Monday, July 27, 2026 · prepared for the Committee on Elders

What this is

We are building a small set of community services — starting with a resident shuttle — run on systems the community can inspect, with every decision recorded and reviewed. Nothing here is a sales pitch: no money is being collected, nothing is being sold, and the whole program stays in rehearsal until it passes legal review and has your consent. REHEARSAL · LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

What is real today (not projections)

  • A dedicated vehicle has been purchased: a 2022 Hyundai Ioniq 5 (electric), bought this month for $24,440.
  • The supporting systems are built and running: scheduling, record-keeping, and a review trail that shows who approved what and when.
  • Every published number is labeled either ACTUAL (it happened) or MODEL (an estimate with its assumptions written down).

How the shuttle would work for residents

The vehicle is available at set times, for specific functions — for example, scheduled rides for errands, appointments, or community events. Which functions, which days, and which hours is exactly what we are asking the committee to help decide. The driver-and-vehicle model is designed so the service pays the vehicle off, and the surplus goes toward a second vehicle — the program grows only from its own work, not from resident investment.

What stays in rehearsal

  • No tokens or investments are offered or sold. Any future community ownership mechanism comes only after legal review, security review, and community consent.
  • No resident data is collected without a written, plain-language consent process reviewed by this committee.
  • The pilot can be paused or stopped by the community at any point.

What we ask of the Committee on Elders

  • Review this brief and tell us what is unclear or missing.
  • Help choose the shuttle's first functions, days, and hours.
  • Set the reporting you want to see at these Monday / Wednesday / Friday walks — we will bring the actuals each time.
