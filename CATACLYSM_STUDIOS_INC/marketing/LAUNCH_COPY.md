## Appendix — Publication Sequence

**Recommended order (launch day, 09:00 ET kickoff):**

1. **Internal Slack (08:30 ET)** — Team sees it first. Zero external risk, maximum operator alignment. They'll reshare before the external clock starts.
2. **LinkedIn founder post (09:00 ET)** — Primary professional signal for the UN/NGO/enterprise audience. Peak LinkedIn activity window.
3. **X / Twitter thread (09:15 ET)** — 15-minute offset keeps cross-platform audiences from seeing duplicate content in the same feed scroll; captures the tech/builder audience.
4. **Discord community post (10:00 ET)** — Community sees LinkedIn traction building, feels momentum, amplifies it.
5. **Email blast (10:30 ET)** — Deliverability peak window. By now social proof is visible — waitlist clicks convert higher.
6. **Short-form captions (11:00 ET — stagger across IG, TikTok, Threads)** — Adds multi-platform coverage without fragmenting attention from the primary LinkedIn/X signal.
7. **LinkedIn carousel follow-up (Day 2, 09:00 ET)** — Capitalizes on algorithm boost from Day 1 engagement. Different format retains attention without repetition fatigue.
8. **Press pitch (Day 3, individual outreach)** — Lets you cite Day 1-2 traction metrics in the pitch. "We got X in 48 hours — here's the story" is a stronger hook than a cold embargo ask.

**Rationale:** Internal → Professional → Tech → Community → Waitlist → Multi-platform → Sustained-day-2 → Press. Each channel amplifies the next; none cannibalize the primary signal.
